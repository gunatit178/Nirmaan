How we build

Not vibe-coded. Orchestrated.

Agency OS is the system behind Nirmaan's work: a coordinated team of specialised AI agents, each with a defined role, carrying a project from discovery to deployment, with a human approving every key decision.

Start your project See the pipeline →

The honest version

A team of specialists. Not one model guessing.

“AI-built” usually means one model improvising a whole project in a single pass: no review, no specialisation, no way to catch a bad decision before it ships. That's the vibe-coded look, and it shows.

Agency OS breaks a project into the roles a real studio has (product, design, architecture, engineering, QA) and routes work through each of them, with review built into every handoff.

  • Orchestrator

    Breaks the objective into workstreams, assigns the right specialist, and never blindly trusts a single output.

  • Specialist agents

    Product, design, architecture, engineering, QA and more, each with a defined mission and its own quality bar.

  • Quality gates

    Work doesn't advance until it clears a defined gate: requirements, design, architecture, QA.

  • Human approval

    The founder signs off at every key gate. The system proposes; a person decides.

The pipeline

Discovery to deployment, gated at every stage.

Five stages. Nothing crosses a gate until a person has reviewed it and said yes.

  1. 1

    Stage 1: Discovery & requirements

    Product and business-analysis agents turn a vague brief into a precise, sourced requirements spec, with assumptions flagged rather than hidden.

    Requirements gate · human sign-off

  2. 2

    Stage 2: Design & architecture

    Design and architecture agents produce the visual direction and the technical plan, reviewed against each other before either is final.

    Design & architecture gate · human sign-off

  3. 3

    Stage 3: Implementation

    Engineering agents build against the approved plan, not freelancing on design decisions nobody signed off.

  4. 4

    Stage 4: QA & security review

    A dedicated QA pass checks the work against the original requirements before it's ever presented as done.

    QA gate · human sign-off

  5. 5

    Stage 5: Deployment & monitoring

    Once approved, work ships, and stays owned rather than abandoned the moment it goes live.
