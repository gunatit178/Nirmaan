# Ideal customer profile · PROPOSED (validate against real leads)

## Best fit

- Small and mid-sized businesses (roughly 5–200 people) that run on
  WhatsApp, spreadsheets, paper or a patchwork of tools, and feel the cost:
  lost orders, missed follow-ups, double bookings, month-end guesswork.
- A decision-maker who can describe the pain in business terms and sign off
  quickly.
- A problem worth solving: it recurs (daily or weekly), touches revenue or
  customers, and has a clear "solved" state.
- Openness to the simplest solution, which is sometimes not custom software at all.

## Also good

- Founders with an MVP to validate who want scope discipline, not a blank cheque.
- Businesses with an existing but fragile system that needs modernization.

## Poor fit (say no, or refer)

- "Build me an app like X" with no problem behind it.
- Fixed price on an undefined scope, with no appetite for discovery.
- Work where the main value is the lowest possible hourly rate.
- Regulated domains that need certifications Nirmaan doesn't hold (for example
  medical-device software): refer out.

## How the OS helps refine this

Lead source, lost reasons (required when a lead is lost) and, from Phase 3,
margin by project type show which customers are actually profitable and happy.
