# Principles · IMPLEMENTED (enforced where marked)

1. **Problem before solution.** Intake starts with the problem; discovery keeps
   facts and guesses apart. *(Enforced in code: an unconfirmed assumption can't
   become a requirement.)*
2. **The simplest system that safely solves the actual problem.** Complexity
   must earn its place.
3. **Humans decide; agents propose.** Scope, plan, go-live and handover are
   human gates. *(Enforced: stage moves require APPROVED gate records.)*
4. **Evidence, not claims.** "Done" means a test proves it. *(Enforced: evidence
   results are computed from pass/total, not from wording.)*
5. **Scope is a contract.** New asks are change requests with price and time.
   *(Enforced: out-of-scope work can't be marked free, and needs a founder or CTO
   decision.)*
6. **Never claim what isn't true.** No invented clients, stats, "sent" messages
   or metrics. *(Enforced across the forms, the dashboard's "not tracked yet"
   tiles, and the AI cost ledger's refusal to guess.)*
7. **Boring technology by default.** SQLite before Postgres, one app before
   services, no paid tool without a reason (see `engineering/procurement.md`).
8. **Every project should teach the next one.** Post-mortems, reusable assets
   and estimate-versus-actual feed back into pricing and delivery.
9. **Private by default.** Client data, economics and internal architecture
   never reach public surfaces.
