# Cost governance · IMPLEMENTED (AI) / PROPOSED (tools and infrastructure)

- **AI spend:** metered per call in the `AiUsage` ledger (see
  `ai/ai-cost-governance.md`). Visible on the Today screen and the AI usage
  screen.
- **Tools and infrastructure:** every paid service goes through the
  procurement questions in `engineering/procurement.md` before purchase, and
  is recorded with its monthly cost and owner. PROPOSED: a simple cost register
  in the OS (Phase 3).
- **Per-project infrastructure** is estimated on the proposal and, from Phase 3,
  recorded as actuals.
