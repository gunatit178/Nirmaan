# Margin model · PROPOSED (targets to be set by the founder, in the OS)

- **Gross margin per project** = (revenue − direct cost) ÷ revenue, where direct
  cost is human effort at a loaded internal rate, plus AI/API, infrastructure
  and other project-specific costs.
- **Floor:** a proposal whose expected margin falls below the founder's floor
  needs a reason (strategic client, reusable IP, portfolio value), recorded
  as a note.
- **Where margin comes from over time:** reusable components (less effort),
  AI-assisted delivery (less effort per feature, but metered, see
  `ai/ai-cost-governance.md`), and recurring plans (low-cost, predictable revenue).
- **Watch:** margin erosion from unpriced change requests (prevented by the CR
  rule), underestimated discovery, and AI spend on low-value tasks (routed to
  cheaper models).

The actual floor and targets are deliberately not written here, because this
repository is public.
