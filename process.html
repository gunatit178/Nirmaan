How We Work

A clear process.
No surprises.

Every project follows a structured approach — from understanding your goals through to launch and ongoing support. You'll always know where things stand.

01

Step 01

Discover

We begin by listening. Before any wireframe or line of code, we want to deeply understand your business, your audience, your goals — and what success actually looks like for this project.

What we learn:

  • Your business model and objectives
  • Your target audience and their needs
  • Current problems you want to solve
  • Timeline and budget expectations

How we do it:

  • Initial consultation call or chat
  • Discovery questionnaire
  • Competitor and market review
  • Requirements documentation
02

Step 02

Plan

Based on what we've learned, we map out the full structure of the project — which pages or screens are needed, what features to build, and which technologies to use. You review and approve before anything starts.

What gets defined:

  • Sitemap / screen architecture
  • Feature list and priorities
  • Technology stack selection
  • Project timeline & milestones

Deliverables:

  • Project scope document
  • Technical proposal
  • Cost estimate
  • Your approval before we proceed
03

Step 03

Design

We create the visual design of your website or application — layout, typography, colour, components. The goal is always clarity and conversion, not decoration for its own sake. You provide feedback and sign off before development begins.

What we design:

  • Page layouts & wireframes
  • Visual design & brand alignment
  • Mobile & desktop views
  • Navigation & user flow

Our approach:

  • Conversion-focused layout
  • Clean & modern aesthetics
  • Feedback rounds included
  • Your approval before building
04

Step 04

Build

The development phase. We build the website, backend, integrations, and any custom functionality defined in the plan. Code is written cleanly, tested thoroughly, and documented so it can be maintained and extended.

Frontend

Interface, pages, responsive layout

Backend

APIs, logic, integrations

Database

Data layer, schema, queries

05

Step 05

Launch & Support

We deploy your project to a live server, run final checks, and make sure everything works as expected across all devices and browsers. After launch, we remain available for ongoing support, updates, and improvements.

At launch:

  • Server deployment & configuration
  • Domain & SSL setup
  • Cross-device & browser testing
  • Performance checks

After launch:

  • Ongoing maintenance
  • Content updates on request
  • New features as you grow
  • Priority support access
